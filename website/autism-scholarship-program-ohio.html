The basics

What the Ohio Autism Scholarship is.

The Ohio Autism Scholarship Program (ASP) is run by the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce. It gives eligible children a scholarship to receive special education services through a private or alternative provider that the family chooses. The award is up to $34,000 per school year.

ABA therapy

One-on-one Applied Behavior Analysis at our center, working on your child’s IEP goals.

Speech-language therapy

Speech-language therapy can be covered by the scholarship. Our speech-language therapists are registered for in-person and virtual speech services.

Education, alongside ABA

A half day of education in small groups with children at a similar level, working on IEP goals and grade-level goals. It’s offered only together with ABA.

Eligibility

Does your child qualify?

The Ohio Department of Education and Workforce sets the rules. In short, here is what the state requires.

Call Columbus (614) 681-1030
  1. 1

    Lives in Ohio

    Your child lives in Ohio, and their IEP comes from your district of residence.

  2. 2

    Autism, identified

    Either your school district has identified your child as a child with autism, or your child has an autism diagnosis from an Ohio-based provider that is reflected in their IEP or Autism Education Plan (AEP). A diagnosis from an out-of-state provider does not qualify.

  3. 3

    A current, finalized IEP

    The IEP must be current and finalized, and all parties, including you, must agree with it.

  4. 4

    Ages 3 through 21

    A child can apply once they turn three, and is age-eligible until their 22nd birthday.

The application

Getting started, step by step.

Here’s how starting the scholarship with On Target works.

01

Talk to us

Call our Columbus line at (614) 681-1030 or send us a message, and ask about the Autism Scholarship.

02

Gather documents

The scholarship application signed by you, your child’s birth certificate, proof of address (a utility bill under 90 days old in your name, not a cell phone bill), and your child’s IEP or diagnostic report. The IEP must be signed on every required line, with the ETR included when one is submitted.

03

Choose your provider

Ohio asks parents to enroll with a participating Autism Scholarship provider. Choose On Target ABA, and we file the application with the state.

04

Start services

Services funded by the scholarship start once the state approves the award. We bill the state directly, each month.

Questions for the state?

The Ohio Department of Education and Workforce runs the program. Its Autism Scholarship office is at autismscholarship@education.ohio.gov, 614-728-3480 or 877-644-6338. Read the official Autism Scholarship page.

ASP FAQ

Questions Ohio parents actually ask.

Is the Autism Scholarship insurance?

No. It’s a state scholarship from the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce. It’s separate from any insurance plan your family has, and it doesn’t require insurance.

What if my child doesn’t have an IEP yet?

Ohio requires either a finalized IEP from your district identifying your child as a child with autism, or an autism diagnosis from an Ohio-based provider reflected in an IEP or an Autism Education Plan (AEP). The state’s Autism Scholarship office can answer questions about your child’s situation at 614-728-3480 or 877-644-6338.

Does the scholarship cover the full cost?

The award is up to $34,000 per school year. There is no out-of-pocket cost to the family for scholarship-funded services.

Is the Autism Scholarship available in Utah?

No. The Autism Scholarship is an Ohio program. It does not apply at our Utah center.
