Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept Medicaid?

Yes! We accept several Medicaid and major insurance plans. Our team will help verify your coverage.

How soon can my child begin therapy?

Start times depend on availability and insurance authorization. We'll work with you to get started as quickly as possible.

What ages do you serve?

We primarily serve children ages 12 and under.

How much does ABA therapy cost?

ABA therapy is covered by many insurance plans. Our team will help you understand your coverage and any out-of-pocket costs.

Does my child need an autism diagnosis?

An autism diagnosis is generally required for insurance-funded ABA therapy. If your child doesn't have a diagnosis, we can help guide you through the process.

Do you offer in-home ABA therapy?

Yes! We offer center-based, in-home and school-based ABA, subject to availability.

Do you offer flexible scheduling?

Yes. We work with families to determine appropriate schedules based on clinical recommendations and availability.

What is ABA therapy?

ABA is an evidence-based therapy that helps children develop communication, social and everyday living skills.
