If you’re looking for autism testing in the Salt Lake Valley, chances are you have been seeing things that worry you: tantrums that seem to come from nowhere, few or no words, little eye contact, or a child who pulls your hand toward what they want instead of asking. When should you consider Autism Testing?

Delayed speech

Getting an autism evaluation does not label your child. It provides clarity and direction.

What happens

What happens during an Autism Evaluation?

Our autism testing process for Salt Lake Valley families includes:

  1. 1

    Parent Interview

    We discuss developmental history and concerns in detail.

  2. 2

    Standardized Autism Assessment

    The licensed psychologist uses validated tools to assess communication and behavioral patterns.

  3. 3

    Structured Observation

    Carefully guided interaction allows for accurate developmental evaluation.

  4. 4

    Written Diagnostic Report

    You receive a clear, detailed autism diagnosis report if criteria are met.

  5. 5

    Clear Next Steps

    We explain recommended services and how to move forward.
