## CHILL ACT Expo Salt Lake City 2026: Exhibitor Guide to Building Local Trust

In a digital landscape crowded with targeted ads, online algorithms, and social media scrolls, building genuine, authentic trust with local families can feel like an uphill battle. This is especially true for providers in the special needs, homeschooling, and behavioral health spaces. Parents looking for support for their children aren’t looking for standard corporate marketing; they are looking for real human connections, transparent practitioners, and verified experts who truly understand their unique daily routines.

That is exactly why the [CHILL ACT Expo](https://chillactexpo.com/) has become America’s fastest-growing special needs and neurodiversity event. On Saturday, August 8, 2026, this major statewide platform is taking over Halls 2 & 3 at the Salt Palace Convention Center in downtown Salt Lake City.

Because admission is completely free for families, caregivers, and kids, thousands of local attendees show up actively looking for autism therapy (ABA), speech pathology, occupational therapy, adaptive sports, and educational planning.

If your organization is ready to show up, connect, and make an impact, your first step is choosing the right presence on the floor. ## 4 Reasons Why In-Person Exhibiting Beats Digital Marketing

When you sign up to become an exhibitor at the CHILL ACT Salt Lake City Expo, you aren’t just renting square footage on a convention hall floor. You are accessing a highly optimized, high-intent audience.

### 1. Families Are Actively Looking for You

Unlike digital ads that interrupt a user’s feed, families attend this event with distinct intentionality. They are there to actively find resources, compare therapeutic methodologies, and discover local tools they didn’t even know existed. You aren’t an interruption; you are the exact answer they came to find.

### 2. High-Trust, Face-to-Face Conversations

An entire day of unhurried, face-to-face interaction builds a level of trust that an email sequence or a search ad simply cannot replicate]. Parents can speak directly to your therapists, meet your intake directors, and ask questions about your programs in a single conversation.

### 3. Highly Visible, Strategic Layouts

Your booth placement is map-optimized, ensuring you are situated precisely where families looking for your specific services head first. This ensures your organization is physically found, rather than being quickly scrolled past or filtered out by an online search engine.

### 4. Lingering Brand Reach

The exposure doesn’t stop when the convention hall lights turn off at 3:00 PM. Your organization’s name, services, and local address go home with families through CHILL ACT’s extensive post-event email marketing campaigns, ongoing social media spotlights, and physical resource guides.

## Choosing the Right Booth Tier for Your Organization

Every price and perk listed on the [CHILL ACT Utah Exhibitor Platform](https://www.chillactutah.com/) is entirely transparent. All core event perks are baked right into the tier prices up front, meaning you never have to worry about unexpected upsells or hidden checkout fees when finalizing your reservation.

Depending on your brand’s size, budget, and marketing goals, you can pick the tier that fits your needs best:

- The Baseline Entry Table: Ideal for emerging local nonprofits, private speech therapists, or smaller specialized homeschool co-ops looking to establish a basic face-to-face footprint on the floor.

- The Standard 10×10 Space: The sweet spot for established local ABA therapy clinics, child development centers, or pediatric dental groups that want plenty of room to set up banners, distribute printed resources, and run interactive children’s activities.

- The Premium Branded 10×20 Booth: Perfect for national provider networks, multi-location healthcare organizations, or private schools looking to make an undeniable, memorable impact on the main floor layout. Higher tiers automatically bundle additional marketing perks, complimentary advertising upgrades, and prime, high-traffic zone placement at no extra cost.

## Maximize Your Salt Lake City Expo Presence

Once you lock in your tier through the CHILL ACT Salt Lake City Booth Portal, you can optimize your booth interaction using these event marketing best practices:

- Provide Free Interactive Mini-Consultations: Families love getting immediate, practical guidance. Offer quick, 5-minute pre-screenings or behavior tool tips to break the ice naturally.

- Incorporate Interactive Activities: The expo features live acrobatics, a petting zoo, and bounce houses to keep children highly engaged. Mirror that fun energy at your booth by offering sensory-friendly games, STEM activities, or prize wheel giveaways.

- Bring Dedicated Intake Staff: Parents are ready to take action. Ensure you have staff on hand who can actively explain your insurance verification processes, detail your open spots, and collect parent emails directly on a tablet to follow up on Monday morning.
