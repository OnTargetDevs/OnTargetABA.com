How to get started

How to Get Started (It’s Easier Than You Think)

We keep the process simple and parent-friendly:

  1. 1

    Reach Out

    Call (385) 550-3500 or send the form. Our intake team calls you back, answers your questions and handles all the paperwork for you before your visit. Want to see our Murray center first? Just ask about a tour.

  2. 2

    Initial Assessment

    You and your child meet with one of our Board Certified Behavior Analysts (BCBAs), who gets to know your child and your family’s goals. No diagnosis yet? We start with an evaluation by one of our in-house psychologists.

  3. 3

    Treatment Plan, Then ABA

    Our BCBAs write your child’s treatment plan, we set a schedule that fits your family, and ABA begins at our Murray center. These steps are usually only a few days apart.
