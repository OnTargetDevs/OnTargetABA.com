## Part 2 — emoji-prefixed post slugs

Four WP posts have a percent-encoded emoji at the front of the slug. On beta the emoji is dropped to a clean slug (the `blog/post.html` renderer strips every non `[a-z0-9-]` char before fetching the markdown, so a literal emoji filename could not be fetched). Old URLs are redirected to the clean slug by SPECIFIC rules placed before the generic `/blog/:slug` rule, plus a `/blog/posts/-{slug}` fallthrough for the renderer's leading-dash sanitisation:

## Known limitations / decisions recorded

- **Body images stripped from imported posts** — matches the 165 pre-existing beta posts (hero + text only) and the only body images on WP were unusable ephemeral Google-Lens URLs. Hero images are downloaded locally (no `ontargetaba.com/wp-content` hotlink), satisfying the grep gate.
- **Pre-existing posts still hotlink the hero** from `ontargetaba.com/wp-content` (165 files, out of scope this run). Recommend a follow-up pass to localise those heroes before the WP box is retired, or they will break after cutover.
- **Emoji old-URL redirects** depend on how CF Pages matches a percent-encoded `_redirects` source; verified against the branch preview where possible. If a given emoji URL does not match, the post is still fully reachable at its clean `/blog/posts/{slug}` URL and on the blog listing.
